The default integration fetches this exact upstream source at runtime:

```text
Repository: https://github.com/RealAhmedOsama/Luna-Forge.git
Version: 2.2.1
Commit: ff37273ba761195ef5ab338d2e90ef3408ce8d8c
```

Luna Forge source is not redistributed in Codex Armada. Its license and notices are obtained from the validated upstream checkout. Users who override the source pin are responsible for reviewing the selected source and license.
